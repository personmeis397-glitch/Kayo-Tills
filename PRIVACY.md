# Privacy Policy

Kayo Tills does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
- The app requests no Android permissions and has no internet access.
- Your settings (voice, volume, reverb, label style) are saved only on your device
  and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Questions? Open an issue on this repository.
