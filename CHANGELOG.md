## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- First Android release of Kayo Tills: 88-key pocket grand piano.
- Four piano voices, sustain, reverb, note/Hz labels and frequency chart.
- Immersive landscape layout, back button closes open panels, adaptive and themed icon.
- Fully offline with a strict Content-Security-Policy and no permissions.
