Kayo Tills puts a full 88-key grand piano on your phone or tablet.

• All 88 keys (A0 to C8) on one landscape screen
• Four piano voices: Steinway & Sons, Fazioli, Blüthner, C. Bechstein
• Press lower on a key to play louder, glide across keys
• Sustain pedal, volume and reverb
• Note names, Hz labels, and a full frequency chart
• Light and dark themes
• Works completely offline. No ads, no accounts, no permissions.
